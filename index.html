🎬 Record & Review studio

Vinh's #1 method — self-audit, right here
Record a 5-minute improvised talk, leave it for a day, then review it three ways: audio-only (voice), muted video (body), and transcript (filler words). Recordings stay on your device during the session — hit Download to keep them. Nothing is uploaded.

3-way review checklist

1 · Audio only (eyes closed)
Vocal variety — am I monotone or do I use melody?
Pace — too fast when nervous? enough pauses?
Do I finish sentences on a confident downward pitch?
2 · Muted video (watch, no sound)
Posture open & grounded? hands visible & purposeful?
Facial expression alive? eyes engaged?
Any non-functional habits (sway, face-touch, fidget)?
3 · Transcript (text only)
Highlight every "um / uh / like / you know / so"
Where do I ramble or trail off?
Circle 3 power words I could land harder
