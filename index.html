3-way review checklist

Vinh's record-and-review method

Record a 5-minute improvised talk. Don't watch it today — you'll be too harsh. Tomorrow, review it three separate ways:

  1. Audio only (eyes shut) → judge your voice: melody, pace, pauses, pitch.
  2. Muted video → judge your body: posture, gestures, face, nervous tells.
  3. Transcript → judge your words: highlight fillers, rambles, weak words.

Fix ONE thing at a time. Re-record every ~12 days and compare against your Day-1 baseline in the archive.
